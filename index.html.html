01 / The purpose

Re-engagement through meaningful, hands-on learning.

Flip My Ride combined car restoration with life skills, mentoring and career exploration for young people aged 12–24 experiencing barriers to education, employment and community participation.

Delivered by AGA in Western Melbourne, the program supported communities across Brimbank, Melton, Wyndham and Maribyrnong. Public program coverage identifies its funding as the Australian Government’s Safer Communities Fund.

03 / Program experience

A car became the starting point for a different future.

Second-hand vehicles provided a shared practical challenge. Specialist trainers guided participants through restoration and automotive skills, while the wider program built work readiness, wellbeing and community connection.

01 / Referral & intake

Engage schools, police, youth justice and community referral partners. Review eligibility, learning needs, consent and participation requirements.

02 / Practical engagement

Use the restoration of a second-hand car as a shared project, building workshop habits, safe tool use, teamwork and confidence.

03 / Skills & wellbeing

Combine automotive activities with First Aid, financial literacy, résumé writing, mentoring, nutrition, fitness and mental health support.

04 / Individual pathways

Offer career counselling and one-to-one discussions with participants, families and case workers. Connect learners with training and employment networks.

05 / Graduation & giving

Showcase the restored vehicle, celebrate participant achievement and arrange its sale or auction to support a community charity.

06 / Transition & follow-through

Support referrals to pre-apprenticeships, apprenticeships, VDSS, RTOs and field officers, with venture-start support where appropriate.

What learning looked like

Automotive & workshop skills

Vehicle washing, servicing and diagnosis; tyre removal and refitting; wheel balancing; transmission servicing; tool maintenance; safe work practices and responsible disposal. My delivery account also includes panel beating, painting and battery maintenance.

Work & life skills

Résumé writing, goal setting, employability, money management, anger management, nutrition and personal wellbeing. Delivery schedules also include First Aid, industry speakers, site visits and practical mentoring.

Access to participation

Free morning tea and lunch, workwear and PPE, Myki support, coordinated pick-up and drop-off, milestone incentives and graduation resources reduced practical barriers to attendance.

Individual support

Youth worker and mentor involvement, specialist wellbeing sessions, psychological support and referral coordination, plus one-to-one conversations with families and case workers where needed.
